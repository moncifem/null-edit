## Verdict per claim
(a) Theory: first-deviation = (1-p_t) prod p_s is an elementary chain-rule identity; speculative-decoding literature implies it (verification of a copied draft is exactly this, rejection prob 1-p_t for sampling). Not a contribution on its own; novelty only in naming/using it as a distribution. Closest: EfficientEdit, Leviathan/Chen speculative decoding (not opened). Caveat to state: the identity holds for ancestral sampling at the token level; greedy/temperature and the "rewrite may re-converge" case (later edits after deviation) are not captured; first deviation only.
(b) Localization on SWE-bench Verified via hazard map: NOT FOUND. Closest FLARE (generation probs, LiveCodeBench), EfficientEdit (qualitative), Yang/Campos (unconditional). Likely novel as an application; empirical.
(c) File-level P(no edit): NOT FOUND; likely novel but weak prior; length-dependence (prod p_t shrinks with length) must be normalized.
(d) Fixed-point verification: NOT FOUND in this search; adjacent to execution-free verifiers (R4P) and refinement stopping criteria. Most novel and highest risk (copy bias, model may edit anything; "no desire to edit" is not correctness; Self-Refine-style evidence says stability != correctness).
(e) Speed: well-known; prefill-vs-decode asymmetry is standard, and speculative editing exploits exactly it. Do not claim as novel.

## Positioning / must cite
Speculative decoding (Leviathan 2023, Chen 2023), prompt lookup / Predicted Outputs / Fast Apply, EfficientEdit (closest mechanical ancestor), REST; naturalness lineage (Ray, Yang, Campos, LLMAO); FLARE; FGL (concurrent); CoEdPilot/NES for next-edit; GEC over-correction/copy-bias; Self-Refine and R4P for verification. Controls: leakage (SWE-bench memorization makes copy probabilities high everywhere, post-cutoff set essential), pure-copy-instruction control, length normalization, instruction-tuning bias toward copying.
